A Letter For My Friend 🤍

In this huge universe full of endless stories, I’m grateful that our paths crossed. Some people come into our lives quietly, but they leave a beautiful mark that stays forever. You are one of those rare people who make ordinary moments feel special.

Thank you for all the little things — the conversations, the laughter, the support, and the memories we created together. Friendship is not about talking every single day; it is about knowing that someone will still be there whenever you need them.

Life changes, people grow, and paths may become different, but I hope you always remember that you have a friend who truly wishes the best for you.

I don’t know how to express it perfectly, but trust me, I'm not trying to impress you. I always want to make you feel valued, appreciated, and cared for in every possible way.

I can't fix everything, but I promise you won't face it alone. I'll sit with you in the dark, remind you of your light, and stay until your smile slowly finds its way back.

You are never a burden to me. Never.

No matter what version of yourself you are going through, I am staying for all of them. I will always encourage you to follow your dreams and remind you that you are capable.

I can't heal everything, but I'll sit beside you and listen until it doesn't feel heavy anymore.

I'm not going anywhere. I won't leave. I won't give up.

Forever grateful for this friendship. 🤍
